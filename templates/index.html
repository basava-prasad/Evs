How It Works

EcoRoute AI scores every candidate route on four normalised metrics. Preference profiles let you control which factors matter most.

01

Multi-Factor Scoring

Routes ranked by a normalised weighted sum of travel time, fuel, CO ², and congestion. Weights are fully configurable per preference profile.

02

Three Preference Profiles

Fastest prioritises travel time at 60%. Eco-Friendly weights fuel and CO ² at 40% each. Balanced distributes weight equally at 25% per metric.

03

Real and Simulated Modes

The simulated 8-node graph is always available offline for instant demos. Real mode fetches genuine road geometry from OpenStreetMap via OSMnx.

04

Comparative Evaluation

Every result set includes the AI recommendation alongside the shortest-distance and fastest-time conventional baselines for clear academic comparison.
